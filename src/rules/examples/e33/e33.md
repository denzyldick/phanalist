# E0033: intdiv() by Zero

E0033 reports `intdiv()` calls whose divisor can be zero. It is the
`intdiv()`-by-zero member of Phanalist's bounded formal verification family
(E0031-E0043). The family shares a Z3-backed engine that encodes path conditions
and values, so a finding comes with a solver-backed counterexample rather than a
heuristic guess.

## What it checks

- `intdiv($value, 0)` and `intdiv($value, $divisor)` where `$divisor` can be zero
- Symbolic divisors, tracked array elements, and bounded integer ranges
- Guarded calls, including `if`, `switch`, `match`, short-circuit `&&`, and
  `try`/`catch`/`finally` paths
- Constant-return, identity, and PHPDoc method/function summaries

## Model limits

E0033 shares the model limits, loop bounds, and interprocedural behavior
described in [E0031](../e31/e31.md).
