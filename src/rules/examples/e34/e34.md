# E0034: intdiv() Overflow

E0034 reports `intdiv()` calls that overflow for `PHP_INT_MIN / -1`. It is the
overflow member of Phanalist's bounded formal verification family
(E0031-E0042). The family shares a Z3-backed engine that encodes path conditions
and values, so a finding comes with a solver-backed counterexample rather than a
heuristic guess.

## What it checks

- `intdiv(PHP_INT_MIN, -1)` and equivalent constant expressions
- Symbolic dividends and divisors whose feasible values reach the overflow case
- Guarded calls and bounded integer ranges
- Constant-return, identity, and PHPDoc method/function summaries

## Model limits

E0034 shares the model limits, loop bounds, and interprocedural behavior
described in [E0031](../e31/e31.md).
